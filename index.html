01 / About me

I connect the big picture with the details people actually use.

My work spans the structure of a product journey, the decisions inside each screen, and the systems that keep an experience coherent as it grows.

In this portfolio, I show the reasoning behind the interface: what the product needs to enable, how people move through it, and how visual decisions support that path.

02 / How I think

From a messy question to a focused experience.

Product design works best when the problem, the journey, and the interface reinforce one another.

01 / Frame

Understand the problem.

Make the goal, constraints, and unanswered questions visible before choosing a solution.

02 / Connect

Map the journey.

Look at how people discover, decide, complete a task, and return later.

03 / Refine

Design a system.

Use clear patterns and states so the experience holds together beyond one screen.
